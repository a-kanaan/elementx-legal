# Privacy Policy for ElementX

**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026

**ElementX** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ElementX mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect minimal data necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our App functionality and online multiplayer services. 

### Types of Data Collected:
* **Financial Information (Purchase History):** When you purchase optional subscriptions (such as premium monthly access for AI and online features), transactions are processed securely through the native app store billing providers (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store). We collect purchase history data to verify your active subscription status.
* **App Performance & Crash Logs:** We use third-party crash reporting tools (such as Sentry) to automatically collect diagnostics and crash logs when the application encounters an error. This helps us diagnose bugs and improve app stability.
* **Device or Other IDs:** We generate and utilize an anonymous local device identifier to save local game preferences, maintain gameplay states, and coordinate peer-to-peer or server-mediated online matchmaking sessions.

## 2. Use of Data
ElementX uses the collected data for the following purposes:
* To provide and maintain core app functionality, including online matchmaking and game engines.
* To process and verify digital subscriptions and in-app purchases.
* To monitor app performance, detect, prevent, and address technical issues via crash analytics.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We **do not** sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. Data is only shared with trusted service providers under strict privacy agreements strictly for the operational purposes outlined above (e.g., Sentry for crash monitoring and Google Play/Apple for secure billing processing). All data transmitted from the app is encrypted in transit over secure HTTPS connections.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental consent in compliance with applicable regulations.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer support channels.
